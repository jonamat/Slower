Slower! - slow music down and transcribe it by ear

A free and open-source web app for working out music by ear: play a track at any speed from 10% to 150% with the pitch untouched, loop the bar you are stuck on, pin reference notes on the spectrogram and hear them, and mark the spots you care about. It is an alternative to Seventh String Transcribe!, with which it is not affiliated, and it runs entirely in the browser: the audio file never leaves your machine.

  • Variable speed without pitch change, using WSOLA time-stretching
  • Seamless looping over any selection
  • WebGL spectrogram with linear or logarithmic frequency axis
  • Pinned notes named by note or by scale degree in any of the 24 natural keys
  • Lettered markers along the timeline
  • MIT licensed, source on GitHub
